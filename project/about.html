Our Purpose

To empower women around the world to lead the next tech revolution in quantum computing, artificial intelligence, and machine learning.

Our Story

A decade ago, our founder met his wife while they were both working in the Middle East. He was amazed by how many women, like his now wife, were leaving their home countries to work abroad in terrible conditions and risky situations. At first, he wondered why so many women would take such great risks. Then he realized it was because they had no other choice. There was no good way to progress in their home countries. He decided to look for a way to change that.

The answer was training women in tech skills. Many bootcamps offered quick paths to jobs in tech, but they are either really expensive or offer no little support and guidance. Wolf Enterprises is the solution. Quick and affordable with great support throughout the program! Now, women around the world are transforming into leaders in tech in their home countries.
